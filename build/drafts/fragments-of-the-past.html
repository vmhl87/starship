In July, I drafted five poems in the margins of my notepad.

Snapshots. That's all they were meant to be, nothing more. But life is as much about the past as it is the future, no matter how hard we wish it isn't. When the light fades, and the sky turns from red to black, we return home.

Always. And these poems were no different. Weeks flew by, mountains of hours and fields of seconds, until my past self looked so small down the road. But the echoes of my sentimental teenage dreams flew on.

They were neither in the past nor the future, and never had been. In limbo.

But maybe they'll live here.

Out of the five I think these three fit the best together, framing a little window into the past.

asymptotic · victims of circumstance · heavenbells

These two didn't make the cut. But maybe you'll enjoy them anyway.

living · supercritical
