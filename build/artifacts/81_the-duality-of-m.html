I was sitting at my computer about a half an hour ago, working on some English homework, and somewhat unexpectedly received a text from this girl who I kind of (well, more than kind of) like.

And quite literally the moment I saw that she had texted me, the amplitude and frequency of my heartbeats increased significantly, and I got that familiar feeling of nervous excitement marked by the tingling of my wrists.


It's honestly astonishing at a biochemical level everything that happened within my physical being to cause such a reaction. To have the physical connection between what is an extraordinarily high-level construct - the social framework in which friendships and relationships can maintain contact and find meaning using what are visually no more than just incredibly dense rectangular arrays of lights - and the "unsophisticated" limbic systems of our primitive mammal ancestors.

What's more, it's a wholly irrational response when taking everything into consideration.

To think of myself as a rational and logically conformant being, I realize, is utterly ridiculous.
