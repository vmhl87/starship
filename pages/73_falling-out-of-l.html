So fast, so fleeting..

Both this and the last cycle stand out as cycles that terminated not due to any actual ending interaction, instead because of internal forces. (This makes them MK, not HK, though possibility of restart is fairly slim..)


The surrounding circumstances are actually pretty interesting this time. Each cycle endpoint genuinely does mark out an inflection point in my personal life: end-of-cycle in spring 2022 induced not only increased self-awareness with regard to the distinction process, but intermeshed with the host of other mindset-change circumstances that occured during the following summer (revision 2 -> revision 3). Winter 2022 laid the groundwork for a social integration structure that made possible much of what has happened within the last cycle, as well as setting a new precedent internally. Summer & fall 2023 branched out into entirely new sections of the state space that I still haven't fully mapped out. Spring 2023 provided the much-needed activation energy to finally convert much of my latent time into useful work. L2C broke through longstanding internal barriers to establish a new social precedent, as well as (though only realized far later) providing crucially needed backpropagation within the L2 sector. Summer 2024, by indirectly reactivating areas of the state space last traversed in revision 4, uncovered levels of hidden bias and improved my internal model of myself.


And now I stand looking back at a journey whose first step I haven't ever taken, whose memories I've lived through a hundred times, yet never had. The path I forged, whose name I'll never know.

Falling out of love into something far greater. Yet another gift from fate.

I leave with so much more than I could have ever hoped. I leave with conviction, this time genuine. I don't hold regrets; the memories that aren't mine won't weigh me down. Just placed in a different context. But most of all, I leave this timeline with self-awareness. Such a powerful tool. It's like the singularity, isn't it?


Revision six. The end of the beginning. Not the beginning of the end.

Hello, world.
