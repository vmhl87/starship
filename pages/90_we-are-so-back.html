We are so back
Jun 28 2025
9:08 AM

Living is a form of not being sure, not knowing what next or how.
The moment you know how, you begin to die a little.
- Agnes de Mille

Sixteen months of continual incremental motion have brought me over the threshold, to phase point. I'd be lying to say I know quite how or why this happened, or that I have acted entirely in concordance with the underlying frequency.

A harmonic cycle only remains so if it keeps repeating.

But like many other things in life, the Cycle was a pattern only made material by the ebb and flow of time. A transient alignment of falling snowflakes that only temporally dictated our fates.

And under the neon glow of rising stars our intertwined worldlines push against the singularity, and break through. The harmonic Cycle sings its last verse as the kaleidoscope collapses into pristine clarity.

The cacophony subsides to a low hum, and I speak for the first time.

What can I say... We are so back.
